Case Study · AI Product

ClosetAI

An AI wardrobe product that recognizes the clothes you actually own and recommends outfits from that catalog—not from a generic style feed. Computer vision for recognition, embeddings for style-aware matching, plain language to ask.

Visit site ↗
FastAPI Next.js NVIDIA NeMo Computer Vision PostgreSQL

Context

The everyday friction: people own more clothes than they actively use, yet getting dressed takes attention, memory, and a little emotional energy every morning. ClosetAI treats the wardrobe as a product rather than a pile.

What I built

An AI wardrobe platform that ingests clothing items, recognizes them with computer vision, builds a searchable catalog, and generates outfit recommendations from that catalog using embeddings and ranking. Natural language queries make it easier to use than a strict category filter.

Why this approach

Computer vision handles item recognition, embeddings support style-aware matching, and plain language keeps the product approachable. FastAPI and PostgreSQL gave the backend a clean, structured shape while Next.js handled the interface.

Role & scope

Product logic, the recommendation flow, and the end-to-end wardrobe experience from item capture to outfit.

Result

A recommendation engine rooted in someone’s actual wardrobe, cutting decision fatigue without adding complexity to the interface.
